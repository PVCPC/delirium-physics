skate-core, skate-data and skate-host are vendored from the Skate 3 Rust Engine
(github.com/SK8-ENGINE/skate-3-rust-engine), as carried by chasmlol's 2010-rust-rewrite-mashup
(engine commit cb7968930f14dad38457e98720d1a274e469eec2).
Copyright (c) 2026 dumbad and the Skate 3 Rust Engine contributors. GPL-3.0-only.

Delirium's changes: trimmed to what Skate 3 mode needs (no network, no zstd maps, no skater mesh check);
Pose carries the trick name, the board's bodies and its spin; hex addresses in comments and constants
scrubbed for the public copy.
No Skate 3 game files are included: they are converted from the user's own disc image at setup.
